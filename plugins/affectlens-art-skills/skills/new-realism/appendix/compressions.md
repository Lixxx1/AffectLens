# Compressions

## Definition And Context

Compressions are mainly associated with Cesar Baldaccini. The artist used mechanical pressure to compress cars, metal scrap, or industrial objects into dense blocks, turning consumer-era product remains into sculpture. Centre Pompidou's Compression "Ricard" can serve as a concrete reference for Cesar-like compressed cars and industrial objects. [Centre Pompidou](https://www.centrepompidou.fr/en/ressources/oeuvre/epDgkqa) These works preserve real material while displaying traces of external force. The form is generated by pressure, material choice, accident, folds, and object history, not by traditional hand modeling.

## Visual Identification

Look for compressed car bodies, metal sheets, mechanical parts, paint layers, and folded edges. Compressions often appear as blocks, columns, or compact masses with dents, wrinkles, rupture, rust, remaining car paint, and metallic reflection. The color may come from original objects: car-paint red, blue, white, or silver mixed with rust, gray-black, and shadow. They are not smooth traditional metal sculpture; violence, pressure, and discarded-object state are the visual evidence.

## Emotion: Valence / Arousal

Arousal is usually high because viewers sense crushing, collision, industrial force, and irreversible destruction. Valence is often negative or mixed: metal weight, wrecked car bodies, and broken surfaces bring heaviness, shock, or accident feeling; bright color and compact form may also create cold humor or strange beauty. Explain that the tension comes from real consumer objects being forcibly transformed and yet still recognizable.

## Common Misreadings

Compressions can be mistaken for abstract metal sculpture. The distinction is material source and process: visible car or industrial remnants crushed by pressure belong closer to Cesar-like compression. Centre Pompidou and National Galleries of Scotland resources on New Realism can help define the boundary of real objects entering art. [Centre Pompidou](https://www.centrepompidou.fr/fr/programme/agenda/evenement/c9nBEXa) [National Galleries of Scotland](https://www.nationalgalleries.org/art-and-artists/glossary-terms/new-realism) If the form is welded, polished, or cast and material origin is not central, it may be modern sculpture rather than Nouveau Realisme compression.
