# Naturalist Landscape

Naturalist landscape focuses on believable natural environment, weather, ground surface, vegetation, sky, light, and local conditions. The Art Story's Naturalism overview can be used as a reference for Naturalism's attention to observable environment and real conditions. [The Art Story](https://www.theartstory.org/movement/naturalism/) It differs from Romantic sublime landscape because it avoids theatricalizing nature, and from Impressionism because it does not primarily dissolve the scene into transient light and subjective color.

When identifying it, ask whether the landscape feels concrete rather than exaggerated. Forests, fields, riverbanks, coasts, slopes, farmhouses, roads, and skies are rendered with careful attention. Colors often include earth tones, gray-blue, soft greens, and natural light. Encyclopedia.com's realism and naturalism resource can be used as background for linking environment, heredity or social conditions, and observational reality. [Encyclopedia.com](https://www.encyclopedia.com/history/encyclopedias-almanacs-transcripts-and-maps/realism-and-naturalism) Composition does not need grandeur; it may present an ordinary place with convincing climate and space. If figures appear, they are usually small and embedded in the environment.

Emotionally, Naturalist landscape is often low to medium arousal. Soft light, broad horizons, and stable composition create calm, plainness, or nostalgia; overcast skies, barren ground, winter, or lonely roads make valence more melancholy or solitary. Tretyakov Gallery's Peredvizhniki resource can be used as a reference for landscape connected to place, social environment, and national experience. [Tretyakov Gallery](https://www.tretyakovgallery.ru/exhibitions/o/peredvizhniki-/?lang=en) If storms or harsh backlighting appear, arousal rises, but judge whether they serve real environment rather than Romantic drama.
