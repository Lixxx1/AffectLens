# Grant Wood Regionalism

Grant Wood's direction emphasizes crisp forms, frontal figures, rural architecture, controlled pattern, and a mix of affection and satire. The emotional tone can feel orderly, restrained, nostalgic, or quietly strange.

Valence is often mixed: stability and local pride coexist with stiffness, social pressure, or irony. Arousal is usually low to medium because composition is controlled and still.
