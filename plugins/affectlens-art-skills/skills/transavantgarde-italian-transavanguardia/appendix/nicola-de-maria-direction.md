# Nicola De Maria Direction

## Definition and Context

Within Transavanguardia, Nicola De Maria leans toward color environments and lyrical abstraction. Compared with the mythic figuration of Chia, Cucchi, or Paladino, De Maria more often builds spiritual spaces through bright color, wall-like expansion, poetic titles, and lighter abstract structures.

This shows that Transavanguardia is not only dark, rough figuration; it can also include a brighter and more lyrical return to painting.

## Image Identification

Look for high-brightness colors, open color areas, mural-like expansion, light lines, abstract signs, and poetic space. The image may lack obvious figures but still emphasize sensuous and spiritual painting rather than detached formal abstraction.

Color is not a cold geometric system; it behaves more like an emotional environment.

## Emotion and Valence/Arousal

Valence is often more positive or neutral: bright, light, lyrical, spiritual, and open. Arousal is usually low to medium, though strong color contrast can raise liveliness.

Avoid assuming Transavanguardia must be dark or violent. This direction asks us to read how color creates a warm but energized painterly field.

## Misclassification Warning

De Maria can be confused with Lyrical Abstraction or Color Field painting. Distinguish by Transavanguardia context, the return to painting, and postmodern spiritual space rather than pure color-plane abstraction. If there is no Italian Transavanguardia context, keep the judgment tentative.
