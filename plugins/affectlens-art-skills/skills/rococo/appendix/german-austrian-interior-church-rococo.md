# German and Austrian Interior / Church Rococo

This direction applies Rococo ornament to interiors and churches: pale stucco, gilding, shell forms, frescoed ceilings, light-filled spaces, and swirling decorative unity. The effect can be airy, theatrical, devotional, and immersive.

Valence is often positive through brightness, elevation, and delicacy. Arousal is medium when ornament fills the space and draws the eye upward. Distinguish it from Baroque church space by lighter color, more playful ornament, and a floating rather than heavy drama.
