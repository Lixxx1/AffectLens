# Boucher / Sensual Mythology

This direction uses mythological subjects as a setting for sensuality, softness, and decorative fantasy. Nymphs, goddesses, cupids, clouds, flowers, silk, and pale flesh create a luxurious and eroticized atmosphere.

Valence is often positive in terms of pleasure and beauty, though it may become ethically complex through objectifying gaze. Arousal is medium when erotic posture, flesh, and ornament are prominent. Distinguish it from Baroque mythology by its lighter color, softness, and intimate decorative tone.
