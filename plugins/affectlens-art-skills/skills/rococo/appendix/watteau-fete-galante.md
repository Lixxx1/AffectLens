# Watteau / Fete Galante

This direction centers on refined outdoor gatherings, music, theatrical costume, courtship, and melancholy leisure. Figures often gather in dreamy gardens or stage-like landscapes. The mood is elegant and pleasurable, but may contain distance, nostalgia, or emotional uncertainty.

Valence is usually positive or gently mixed. Arousal is low to medium because gestures are restrained and atmosphere is soft. Look for muted pastels, delicate figures, theatrical poses, and a sense that pleasure is temporary.
