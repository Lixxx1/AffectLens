# Fragonard / Flirtatious Narrative

This direction emphasizes witty, fast, flirtatious scenes with theatrical movement and intimate secrecy. Gardens, swings, hidden observers, letters, flowers, and playful gestures often create a sense of desire and social game.

Valence is usually lively and positive, with possible unease if voyeurism or manipulation is strong. Arousal is medium because movement, diagonals, foliage, and narrative suspense animate the scene. The emotional key is pleasure mixed with performance.
