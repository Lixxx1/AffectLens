# Color Field Painting

Color Field Painting is a branch of Abstract Expressionism that emerged in the late 1940s and was developed by artists such as Mark Rothko, Barnett Newman, and Clyfford Still[\[1\]](https://www.theartstory.org/movement/color-field-painting/#:~:text=Summary%20of%20Color%20Field%20Painting). Unlike Action Painting, its artists rejected overt figuration and covered the canvas with large areas of unified or gradually shifting color, allowing color itself to stir the viewer's emotions.

## Key Characteristics

- **Large areas of color**: Broad fields of color or gradients cover the canvas, usually without clearly defined shapes or outlines[\[1\]](https://www.theartstory.org/movement/color-field-painting/#:~:text=Summary%20of%20Color%20Field%20Painting).
- **Immersive color**: Color surrounds the viewer, creating an experience of contemplation and transcendence beyond ordinary reality[\[1\]](https://www.theartstory.org/movement/color-field-painting/#:~:text=Summary%20of%20Color%20Field%20Painting).
- **Unity of form and content**: These works remove the distinction between subject and background; the color field itself forms the work's space and content[\[2\]](https://www.theartstory.org/movement/color-field-painting/#:~:text=,spread%20out%20beyond%20the%20edges).
- **Simplification and meditation**: The paintings emphasize relationships among colors and their psychological effects, creating a feeling akin to modern myth[\[1\]](https://www.theartstory.org/movement/color-field-painting/#:~:text=Summary%20of%20Color%20Field%20Painting).

## Representative Artists

- **Mark Rothko**: Explored emotion through floating rectangular fields of color, allowing colors to merge and evoke a spiritual experience[\[1\]](https://www.theartstory.org/movement/color-field-painting/#:~:text=Summary%20of%20Color%20Field%20Painting).
- **Barnett Newman**: Added long, slender "zip" lines within expansive fields of pure color to emphasize a sense of space[\[3\]](https://www.theartstory.org/movement/color-field-painting/#:~:text=Saying%20,new%20way%20to%20experience%20space).
- **Clyfford Still**: Used thickly applied, palette-knife-like fields of color to create torn, nature-like imagery[\[4\]](https://www.theartstory.org/movement/color-field-painting/#:~:text=late%201940s,colors%2C%20from%20edge%20to%20edge).
- **Helen Frankenthaler**: Pioneered later Color Field painting by soaking diluted pigment into untreated canvas[\[5\]](https://www.theartstory.org/movement/color-field-painting/#:~:text=Helen%20Frankenthaler%20played%20a%20crucial,Color%20Field%20Painting%20advanced%20further).

## Emotional Tone and Viewing Tips

Color Field Painting guides emotion through color itself, often conveying contemplative, mysterious, or transcendent experiences. When analyzing these works, pay attention to the size of the color blocks, the softness of their edges, and the interaction between color fields; consider how these qualities shape your mood and level of alertness. Because there is no figurative structure, the viewer's path through the painting is guided mainly by gradations of color or contrasts between adjacent colors, and it is often comparatively calm.
