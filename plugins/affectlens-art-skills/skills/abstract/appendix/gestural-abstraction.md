# Action/Brushstroke Abstraction (Gestural Abstraction)

Action abstraction or brushwork abstraction is an abstract art form that emphasizes the creative action itself. Fiveable teaching materials point out that action abstraction emphasizes dynamic brushstrokes and improvisational expressions in the painting process, focusing on the movements of the artist's hands and the traces of the body on the canvas, rather than reproducing images [\[1\]](https://fiveable.me/art-renaissance-to-modern-times/key-terms/gestural-abstraction#:~:text=Gestural%20abstraction%20is%20an%20art,directness%20in%20the%20viewer%27s%20experience)。AstaGuru The article also points out that it highlights visible brushstrokes, splatters and dynamic energy, and is closely related to "Action Painting" in abstract expressionism[\[2\]](https://www.astaguru.com/blogs/4-main-types-of-abstract-art-that-changed-the-art-world-567#:~:text=3)。

## Key Features

- **Dynamic brushstrokes and dripping**: Paint is applied by brushing, scraping, splashing, dripping, etc., leaving obvious traces of movement[\[1\]](https://fiveable.me/art-renaissance-to-modern-times/key-terms/gestural-abstraction#:~:text=Gestural%20abstraction%20is%20an%20art,directness%20in%20the%20viewer%27s%20experience)。
- **Improvisation and Energy**: Creation emphasizes speed and physicality, composition is often unpredictable, and conveys immediate emotional bursts[\[2\]](https://www.astaguru.com/blogs/4-main-types-of-abstract-art-that-changed-the-art-world-567#:~:text=3)。
- **Large Canvas**: In order to allow the body movements to fully unfold, the size of the work is usually larger [\[3\]](https://fiveable.me/art-renaissance-to-modern-times/key-terms/gestural-abstraction#:~:text=presence%2C%20as%20seen%20in%20the,body%20engagement%20during%20creation)。
- **Emphasis on the process**: The viewer not only pays attention to the completed image, but also feels the rhythm and power of the creative process. Valence may be positive or negative, but arousal is usually higher.

## Represent the artist

- **Jackson Pollock**: Famous for his drip painting technique, standing around the canvas and dripping paint to form a complex network of lines[\[2\]](https://www.astaguru.com/blogs/4-main-types-of-abstract-art-that-changed-the-art-world-567#:~:text=3)。
- **Franz Kline**: Use thick black brushstrokes to create bold structures on a white background[\[2\]](https://www.astaguru.com/blogs/4-main-types-of-abstract-art-that-changed-the-art-world-567#:~:text=3)。

## Emotions and Appreciation Tips

Action abstract works are often full of energy and tension. As you observe, feel the direction, speed, and strength of the brushstrokes, and consider how these physical traces affect your emotions. The reading path may follow the lines and jump randomly, creating a strong arousal.
