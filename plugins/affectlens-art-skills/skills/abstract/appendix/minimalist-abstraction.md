# Minimalist Abstraction

Minimalism is a modern art trend that emerged in the 1960s. It emphasizes removing superfluous elements and using simple shapes, lines and colors to convey power. The AstaGuru art blog states that minimal abstraction "achieves maximum effect by reducing elements to the most basic shapes, lines and colors", often employing monochrome or limited tones and large areas of negative space to create a calm and contemplative atmosphere[\[1\]](https://www.astaguru.com/blogs/4-main-types-of-abstract-art-that-changed-the-art-world-567#:~:text=o%20Definition%3A%20Minimalist%20abstraction%20focuses,of%20calm%2C%20space%2C%20and%20contemplation)。

## Key Features

- **Minimalist Elements**: Use simple geometric shapes (such as squares, strips) and straight lines, and avoid complex shapes or decorations[\[1\]](https://www.astaguru.com/blogs/4-main-types-of-abstract-art-that-changed-the-art-world-567#:~:text=o%20Definition%3A%20Minimalist%20abstraction%20focuses,of%20calm%2C%20space%2C%20and%20contemplation)。
- **Single color or limited color tone**: The picture is often composed of a single color tone or several similar colors to reduce visual noise [\[1\]](https://www.astaguru.com/blogs/4-main-types-of-abstract-art-that-changed-the-art-world-567#:~:text=o%20Definition%3A%20Minimalist%20abstraction%20focuses,of%20calm%2C%20space%2C%20and%20contemplation)。
- **Large area of ​​negative space**: Blank or blank areas occupy an important position, allowing the viewer to focus on a small number of elements and create a sense of meditation[\[1\]](https://www.astaguru.com/blogs/4-main-types-of-abstract-art-that-changed-the-art-world-567#:~:text=o%20Definition%3A%20Minimalist%20abstraction%20focuses,of%20calm%2C%20space%2C%20and%20contemplation)。
- **Precision and restraint**: simple form but meticulous construction, emphasizing the overall proportion, scale and materials of the work. Emotional expression is reserved, valence is usually neutral or positive, and arousal is low.

## Represent the artist

- **Frank Stella**: Advocating the concept of "what you see is what you see", his works use hard-edged lines and solid colors[\[2\]](https://www.astaguru.com/blogs/4-main-types-of-abstract-art-that-changed-the-art-world-567#:~:text=o%20Famous%20Examples%3A%20%E2%80%A2%C2%A0%C2%A0%20%C2%A0Frank,grids%20and%20soft%20color%20washes)。
- **Agnes Martin**: Known for her delicate grids and soft tones, her works present a meditative tranquility[\[2\]](https://www.astaguru.com/blogs/4-main-types-of-abstract-art-that-changed-the-art-world-567#:~:text=o%20Famous%20Examples%3A%20%E2%80%A2%C2%A0%C2%A0%20%C2%A0Frank,grids%20and%20soft%20color%20washes)。

## Emotions and Appreciation Tips

Minimalist abstraction encourages the viewer to slow down and appreciate subtle tonal changes, material textures and spatial relationships. As you look at the work, think about the proportions of white space and shape, and how simple structure affects your state of mind.
