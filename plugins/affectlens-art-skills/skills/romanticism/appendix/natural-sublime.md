# Natural Sublime

The natural sublime emphasizes awe, terror, vast scale, and the smallness of human beings before mountains, seas, storms, fog, fire, glaciers, or deep sky. The viewer should feel that nature exceeds ordinary control.

Valence can be positive through awe and transcendence, negative through danger and helplessness, or mixed. Arousal is usually medium to high because scale, weather, and unstable light create pressure.

Distinguish this direction from ordinary landscape painting by the sense of overwhelming power. The landscape should feel emotionally or spiritually immense, not merely scenic.
