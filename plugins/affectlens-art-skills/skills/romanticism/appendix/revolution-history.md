# Revolution / History

This direction uses political upheaval, battle, national memory, martyrdom, heroic bodies, crowds, flags, smoke, fire, and dramatic gesture. Romantic history painting often transforms public events into scenes of passion, sacrifice, violence, and liberty.

Valence is usually mixed: courage, hope, and liberation coexist with death, grief, and fear. Arousal is high because of motion, conflict, strong diagonals, crowded bodies, and dramatic light.

Distinguish it from Neoclassical history painting by its emotional turbulence, painterly drama, and focus on passion rather than restrained moral order.
