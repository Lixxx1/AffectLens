# Pastoral Nature

The pastoral direction emphasizes rural refuge, memory, simplicity, and harmony with nature. It may show shepherds, fields, trees, cottages, evening light, animals, or gentle weather. The mood is less about overwhelming terror and more about longing, innocence, and retreat.

Valence is often positive or gently melancholic. Arousal is usually low to medium, shaped by soft light, open space, and slow rhythm. If the scene includes ruins or fading light, nostalgia may complicate the mood.

Distinguish it from ordinary picturesque landscape by the intensity of longing and emotional projection onto nature.
