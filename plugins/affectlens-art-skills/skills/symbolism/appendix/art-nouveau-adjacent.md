# Art Nouveau Adjacent

Symbolism often overlaps with Art Nouveau through sinuous line, ornamental surface, flowers, hair, female figures, and stylized nature. In this adjacent direction, the line itself becomes emotionally charged: flowing, hypnotic, sensual, or uncanny.

Look for elongated plants, halo-like circles, patterned backgrounds, elegant but strange figures, and decorative rhythms that make the image feel like an icon or dream. Valence can be beautiful and seductive, but may become uneasy when the figure appears trapped, fatal, or inhumanly stylized. Arousal is usually controlled, rising through repetition, gaze, and serpentine line.

Distinguish this direction from purely decorative Art Nouveau by asking whether the image carries symbolic ambiguity, psychological tension, or spiritual suggestion.
