# French Symbolism

French Symbolism is strongly tied to poetry, dream, myth, decadence, and spiritual suggestion. Works may include pale figures, mythological scenes, flowers, sphinxes, angels, femme fatale imagery, and inward-looking faces. The image often feels less like a story and more like a mental climate.

Valence is frequently mixed: beauty and spirituality coexist with death, erotic unease, or melancholy. Arousal is often low to medium when the atmosphere is still, but rises with intense gaze, dark allegory, or threatening myth.

Distinguish this direction from Academic history painting by its ambiguity and inwardness. The subject may look classical or mythic, but the emotional function is suggestive rather than narrative.
