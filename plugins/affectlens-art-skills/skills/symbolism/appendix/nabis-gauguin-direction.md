# Nabis / Gauguin Direction

This direction uses flat color, simplified shapes, decorative composition, spiritualized everyday scenes, and synthetic form. It is related to Gauguin's influence and the Nabis' interest in painting as a surface of color, rhythm, memory, and belief.

Look for outlined forms, flattened perspective, patterned interiors, quiet figures, symbolic color, and scenes that feel devotional or dreamlike rather than descriptive. Valence may be calm, nostalgic, mysterious, or spiritually charged. Arousal is usually low to medium unless color contrast is intense.

Distinguish it from Post-Impressionism by asking whether color and flatness serve symbolic or spiritual suggestion rather than only optical or structural experiment.
