# Modern Realism

Modern Realism adapts realist observation to twentieth-century and contemporary conditions. It may depict city life, domestic spaces, alienation, social change, industrial environments, or ordinary bodies using modern composition and sometimes simplified form.

Valence can range from calm familiarity to loneliness or critique. Arousal is usually low to medium, shaped by composition, scale, and social tension. The key is that recognizability serves observation of modern life rather than nostalgia or fantasy.

Distinguish it from Social Realism by whether the political or class critique is central.
