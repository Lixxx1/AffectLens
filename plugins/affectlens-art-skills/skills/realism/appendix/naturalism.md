# Naturalism

Naturalism is a realist-related direction that emphasizes close observation of environment, light, behavior, and social or biological conditions. It may feel more observational, empirical, and deterministic than broader Realism.

Look for rural labor, landscape conditions, weather, bodies shaped by work, and detailed surroundings. Valence is often neutral or mixed, and arousal is usually restrained unless the subject includes hardship or dramatic environment.

Distinguish Naturalism from Impressionism by its interest in social and environmental truth rather than optical sensation alone.
