# French Realism

French Realism is associated with artists such as Courbet, Millet, and Daumier. It emphasizes contemporary life, labor, peasants, workers, and social observation rather than mythological or aristocratic subjects.

Look for rougher surfaces, ordinary bodies, rural labor, urban satire, and unheroic scale. Valence may be neutral, dignified, melancholy, or critical. Arousal is usually low to medium, rising when social injustice or political satire is explicit.

Distinguish this direction from Romanticism by its rejection of dramatic fantasy, and from Academic Art by its refusal to idealize ordinary subjects.
