# North Korean Socialist Realism

North Korean Socialist Realism emphasizes leader imagery, military discipline, collective loyalty, heroic workers, agricultural abundance, national defense, children, bright futures, and highly controlled emotional clarity. The image often combines monumental composition with intense ideological optimism.

Valence is intended to be positive through devotion, unity, strength, and gratitude. Arousal is medium to high through bright color, organized crowds, banners, military posture, and dramatic light. The tone may also feel rigid, theatrical, or coercive because individual emotion is subordinated to collective ideology.

Distinguish it from Soviet and Chinese variants by its especially centralized leader cult, military nationalism, and tightly controlled visual rhetoric.
