# Soviet Socialist Realism

Soviet Socialist Realism emphasizes heroic workers, peasants, soldiers, Lenin or Stalin imagery, industrial progress, collectivized agriculture, science, youth, and the radiant future. Composition is usually clear, monumental, and optimistic.

Valence is intended to be positive through hope, pride, discipline, and collective strength. Arousal is medium to high through upward movement, banners, machinery, crowds, and heroic scale. A critical reading may also detect rigidity, coercion, and ideological pressure.

Distinguish it from nineteenth-century Realism by its idealized state goal and from Social Realism by its affirmative propaganda function.
